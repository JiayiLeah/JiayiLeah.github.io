About Me

I am an undergraduate student in International Economics and Trade at the University of Science and Technology Beijing.

Starting in Fall 2026, I will pursue the Master of Arts in Economics at the Kenneth C. Griffin Department of Economics, University of Chicago.

My current research preparation centers on building strong empirical and computational skills. Through research and applied data projects, I have worked with Python, R, SQL, econometric modeling, machine learning methods, network analysis, and financial time-series data. My recent work has involved fund-level risk and alpha modeling, multi-asset signal evaluation, panel data construction, and reproducible empirical workflows.These experiences have shaped my interest in research questions where economic theory, data, and computation jointly inform market and firm-level decision-making.

I am Ph.D.-oriented and currently exploring research opportunities in two directions:

empirical industrial organization
  • particularly market structure and pricing
financial econometrics
  • especially machine learning methods for high-dimensional financial and asset-pricing data
