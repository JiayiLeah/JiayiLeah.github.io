Leadership

  • Founder, Gray Sparrow Healthcare Access & Patient Mobility Support Initiative

    Gray Sparrow is a healthcare access initiative I founded to support “medical migrant” families in China: patients and caregivers who travel across cities and provinces for better treatment, but often face unfamiliar hospital systems, fragmented online information, emotional isolation, and limited non-medical support.

    I led a 10-member founding team from zero to one, built partnerships with foundations, hospitals, and patient wards, and designed a transferable three-cohort operating model. Our work combined hospital navigation, bedside companionship, patient education, ward-based activities, and fundraising through online cultural products, offline charity sales, and live-streaming campaigns.

    Beyond direct volunteering, we introduced a data-driven approach to better understand patient needs. Using Python and NLP methods, our team analyzed first-hand patient interviews, volunteer records, and publicly available online healthcare information to identify recurring pain points in hospital navigation, treatment preparation, caregiver support, and information search. These insights helped us refine service priorities, improve the relevance of our patient-support materials, and make hospital-related information easier for families to access and understand.

    To make the initiative sustainable, we built a self-driven fundraising mechanism with transparent donation records. The project has supported 500+ patients and families, expanded across multiple hospitals and regions, and raised over RMB 70,000 in cumulative donations. Over time, Gray Sparrow evolved from a volunteer service into a technology-enabled social impact project, connecting grassroots healthcare needs with structured execution, data analysis, and long-term organizational capacity.
